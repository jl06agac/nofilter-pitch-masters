SECTION 02 · TERMS & CONDITIONS
contract terms
CL. 01 — CL. 08
BINDING · GOVERNED BY THE LAWS OF SINGAPORE
operative · 01—04
CL. 01
Equipment Supply

The Supplier supplies one (1) CaféMatic 5 commercial coffee machine and one (1) compatible milk cooler, as itemised in Schedule A, on a rental basis. The equipment remains the property of the Supplier (or its financing partner under Cl. 10) for the term.

CL. 02
Installation

The Supplier installs and commissions the equipment at the Customer's site on a pre-agreed date with all pre-installation requirements confirmed completed.

CL. 03
Service & Maintenance

The Supplier provides bi-annual preventative maintenance and calibration as part of this rental agreement, and bears the cost of all spare parts changes, save where the fault is caused by Customer negligence. The Customer shall purchase and apply daily cleaning materials at its own cost. Response time for callouts: twenty-four (24) hours.

CL. 04
Consumables Supply

The Supplier supplies coffee beans, cocoa powder, and other consumables on demand, drawn from the menu in Schedule B. The Customer may add or remove items from the menu by written notice; pricing is per Schedule B.

commercial · 05—08
CL. 05
Conservation Contribution

For each kilogram of conservation coffee purchased and paid for by the Customer, the Supplier contributes a percentage of the purchase value to its NGO conservation partners (identified in Schedule D). The applicable rate is set by the agreed coffee price in Schedule D: five percent (5%) where the price is below SGD [[RATE_THRESHOLD]]/kg, and ten percent (10%) at or above SGD [[RATE_THRESHOLD]]/kg. Where the Customer elects a voluntary co-contribution (“Top-Up”), that amount is passed to the NGO partners in full, in addition to the percentage. Contributions are calculated on volume purchased and paid for, reconciled quarterly (31 March, 30 June, 30 September, 31 December), and paid to the NGO partners within sixty (60) days of each reconciliation date. Based on the agreed coffee price, the applicable conservation rate is , with a voluntary Top-Up of /kg.

CL. 06
Pricing

Equipment rental is fixed at the rate set in Schedule A for the term. Consumables are fixed at the rates in Schedule B for the first twelve (12) months from [[TERM_START]], then reviewed annually with thirty (30) days' written notice.

CL. 07
Order Process

Consumables orders are placed by email or messaging to the Supplier contact named in this agreement. Coffee orders are fulfilled within five (5) business days assuming that orders are placed prior to the Monday 1200 weekly deadline.

CL. 08
Payment

Equipment rental is invoiced monthly in advance by our leasing partner (the financing partner under Cl. 10). Consumables are invoiced in arrears by the Supplier. Payment terms: Net 30 days.

CL. 09
Term & Renewal

The term is [[TERM_MONTHS]] months, running from [[TERM_START]] to [[TERM_END]]. Renewal is by mutual written agreement no later than sixty (60) days before expiry. In the absence of agreement, the equipment is collected by the Supplier within ten (10) business days of contract expiry.

governance · 10—14
CL. 10
Financing Partner

The equipment rental component is financed through our leasing partner. The Customer pays our leasing partner directly for equipment rental and may need to onboard our leasing partner as a payee in its accounts payable system. The Supplier remains the Customer's sole point of contact and is operationally and legally responsible for all matters relating to the equipment, service, coffee quality, and consumables.

CL. 11
Title & Risk

Title to the equipment remains with the Supplier or our leasing partner throughout the term. The Supplier bears risk of loss or damage to the equipment in normal use. Damage from misuse or negligence is for the Customer's account at cost.

CL. 12
Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice. The Supplier may also terminate on non-payment exceeding sixty (60) days. On termination, the Customer permits collection of equipment within ten (10) business days.

CL. 13
Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. The Singapore courts have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

CL. 14
GST

All amounts payable under this agreement, including those set out in Schedule A and Schedule B, are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax. GST will be added to invoices at the prevailing rate.

BINDING · OPERATIONAL DETAIL FOR CL. 02 & CL. 03
C.1 Installation requirements

The Customer shall provide the following utilities at the install location, within one (1) metre of the machine:

  • Power — single phase supply (1P/N/E, 230V, 13A) with circuit breaker
  • Water — fixed supply, minimum 2 bar pressure, ½" thread stop cock (If fixed water input).
  • Drainage — 50 DN, with sufficient gradient (If fixed water input and output).

The Supplier installs the machine free of charge and does not perform carpentry, plumbing, or electrical works.

C.2 Out-of-hours callout

Callouts requested outside the pre-arranged quarterly schedule are charged at the prevailing rates:

Mon–Fri, working hours
SGD 150.00plus SGD 50/hr labour thereafter
After hours / weekends / PH
SGD 200.00plus SGD 40/hr labour thereafter

Callouts caused by Customer mishandling, failure to clean, or damage attributable to the Customer are charged at the rates above plus parts at cost.

C.3 Response time

The Supplier shall endeavour to rectify reported faults within twenty-four (24) hours of the request. Where a machine is not fixable on-site, the Supplier shall provide a replacement machine of equivalent capacity by the next working day following the failure date.
