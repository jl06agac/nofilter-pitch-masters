Conservation coffee · Indonesia and Myanmar

Coffee grown on the edge of forests
that are still standing.

NoFilter buys from smallholders farming under canopy in the buffer zones of threatened forest, and pays a contracted share of every sale through to the conservation NGO holding that boundary. Three Sumatran origins are available for supply now. A fourth, from the Arakan Mountains in Myanmar, is possible, but supply there is limited and may run out.

Every listing carries its own seal, in the colour reserved to that origin, so a bag, carton or sample says which coffee it is before it is opened.

Built to sell
on its story.

Most coffee on a workplace shelf is bought where it is cheapest to grow, and cheap usually means cleared ground. This range is the opposite proposition, and it comes with the evidence to say so out loud.

Contracted, not charitable

The conservation share is written into the supply contract and shown on every invoice, so it can be audited rather than taken on trust.

Mapped to a boundary

Every lot ties to a named forest boundary with coordinates and a named NGO partner, held on NoFilter's public verification map.

Documented for reporting

Chain of custody documentation is available for customers carrying Scope 3 or sustainable procurement obligations.

Range and formats

Four single-origin roasts with genuinely different cup profiles, supplied in 1 kg whole bean. Trade pricing, volumes and terms are set out overleaf.

Next step

Trade pricing and terms are overleaf and hold for twelve months. The call is to agree which of the four origins earn a listing first, and when you want the first delivery.

A price that leaves room
