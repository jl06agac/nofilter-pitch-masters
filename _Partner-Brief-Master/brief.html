Built to sell
on its story.

You already sell food from small farms that leave the land better than they found it. This is the coffee that belongs beside it: grown by smallholders under forest canopy in Sumatra and Myanmar, roasted to order in Singapore each week, with a contracted share of every kilo going to the NGO protecting that forest, and the coordinates to prove it.

Every listing carries its own sticker, in the colour reserved to that origin, so a bag, carton or sample says which coffee it is before it is opened.
Contracted, not charitable

The conservation share is written into the supply contract and shown on every invoice, so it can be audited rather than taken on trust.

Mapped to a boundary

Every lot ties to a named forest boundary with coordinates and a named NGO partner, held on NoFilter's public verification map.

Yours to tell

Every bag names its origin, its forest and its NGO partner. You get approved wording, product photography and a per-kilo conservation figure for your own impact page and your boxes.

Range and formats

Four single-origin roasts with genuinely different cup profiles, in 1 kg and 250 g whole bean. Trade pricing and terms are set out overleaf.

Next step

Trade pricing and terms are overleaf and hold to 30 September 2027. The call is to agree which of the four origins earn a listing first, and when you want the first delivery.
