Why this coffee

Mostcoffee is grown
where it’s cheapest.
Ours, where it matters most.

Every coffee we source grows in the agroforestry that hems a forest under pressure — Batang Toru, Leuser, the Arakan highlands — ripening under standing canopy rather than on the bare ground left where forest falls. That agroforestry is the line itself: a living, working edge, tended by the people who farm it, that holds the clearing back. And it only holds while the coffee finds a buyer. So your order is never a gesture — it funds the NGO partners who keep that edge alive, shelters the forest and the flagship species that call it home, and gives the families under the canopy a reason to let the trees stand. Between 5 and 10% of every sale, ring-fenced before anyone takes a margin, goes straight to that work.
