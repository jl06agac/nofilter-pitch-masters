NOFILTERWHO WE ARE

Most coffee begins with
price and taste.
We begin with place.

NoFilter is a conservation-coffee social enterprise, founded in Singapore and now supplying workplaces, hotels and cafés in Singapore and the UAE. We buy only from smallholders farming under shade in the buffer zones of threatened forest, pay them above market to keep that forest standing, and return 5 to 10% of every sale to the conservation NGO holding the boundary. Then we bring the whole programme to your pantry: the coffee, the machines, the servicing, the supplies and the evidence.

01 · COFFEEWHERE IT COMES FROM
Four forests.
Still standing.
01

Every NoFilter coffee comes from smallholders farming under canopy in the buffer zone of a threatened forest, bought through the conservation NGO working there. Farmer income at the forest edge decides whether that forest stands or is cleared, so that is where we put the money.
