§01

At NoFilter, we back one kind of coffee.

That’s why we source from just four landscapes. Each one helping to protect a flagship species and the forest it calls home. [[TARGET_NAME]] chooses which to back. Each coffee helps hold the line.

Batang ToruPRCF · SRI

The Tapanuli orangutan is the rarest great ape on earth — fewer than 800 remain, all in this single forest. With the People Resources and Conservation Foundation (PRCF), NoFilter sources from the smallholders along its edge, keeping the trees between farm and forest standing.

Batang GadisSRI

Coffee from the buffer of Batang Gadis National Park — forest still shared by tigers, tapir and hornbills. With the Sumatra Rainforest Institute, every sale helps the farmers who hold its boundary keep that boundary where it is.

Gayo LuesOIC

The edge of the Leuser Ecosystem — the last place orangutans, tigers, elephants and rhinos still share one landscape. Our partner, the Orangutan Information Centre (OIC), has worked here for two decades, helping communities secure protection over thousands of hectares of forest.

Arakan MountainsFauna & Flora Myanmar

Watershed forest and the western hoolock gibbon, held through coffee grown in the shade rather than cleared land. NoFilter works with Fauna & Flora Myanmar and the Asho Chin Coffee Association, backing habitat and the livelihoods that depend on it.
